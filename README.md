Game: MURP (working title, short for murder puppets)
Members: Javaya Boyd, Jordan Kiryakoza, Seamus Ryan

Description:   (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K-UvdUOy1bPEXEcChm0E2h7_oIMuPOOJF1yGLuu3VUQ/edit?usp=sharing)
  - Survival-Horror-Adjacent Puzzle game inspired by the original Resident Evil games, Silent Hill, and Crow Country
  - Follows a frog puppet traversing the ruins of an old theater where monsters may lurk as he ventures deeper into the property.
  - Gameplay loop may be traversing an eerie area, avoiding monsters and encountering small puzzles sometimes mini-games) that
      allow you to proceed in different ways, like with new items or opening doors. Combat isn't the focal point to reinforce horror,
      though it isn't supposed to be too genuinely scary
  - Assets should resemble story, going for puppet characters, frog main character, wooden doll enemies or cloth-based.
